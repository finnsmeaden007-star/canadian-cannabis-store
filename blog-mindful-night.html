Planning a mindful night in.

BY THE CANADIAN CANNABIS STORE · FOR ADULTS 19+

Sometimes the best plan is the one with fewer plans. A little preparation can help make an at-home cannabis experience more intentional and comfortable.

Set the scene

Pick a familiar, comfortable space. Put on a record, choose a movie, make a snack, or simply leave some room for a good conversation. The goal is not to do more—it is to be present.

Keep it uncomplicated

Choose one product and take your time. Avoid combining substances, make sure you will not be driving, and keep your plans light. Give yourself plenty of time before deciding whether to consume more.

Keep cannabis securely stored and out of reach of children, pets, and anyone under the legal age.

Check in with yourself

If something does not feel right, pause. Have water, get comfortable, and remind yourself that effects pass. If you have concerns about cannabis and your health or medication, speak with a qualified health professional.

This article is for general education only and is not medical advice. Adults 19+ only. Do not drive or operate machinery while impaired.
