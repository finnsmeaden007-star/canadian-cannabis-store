How to read a cannabis label.

BY THE CANADIAN CANNABIS STORE · FOR ADULTS 19+

Cannabis packaging contains a lot of small type. Here are the details worth looking for when you are comparing products on a menu or in store.

Format and quantity

First, identify what the product is and how much it contains: for example, dried flower by weight, a number of pre-rolls, or the number of units in a package. This makes it easier to compare products fairly.

THC and CBD

Labels list THC and CBD content or ranges. These numbers are pieces of information, not a promise of a particular experience. Potency is one factor among many, so it is always okay to ask for help interpreting it.

Use a licensed retailer and check that packaging is sealed, labelled, and clearly identifies the producer.

Packaged-on date

Look for the packaging date and storage information. Keeping products in their original packaging and away from children and pets is important.
