CANNABIS 101 · 4 MIN READ

A relaxed guide to finding your footing.

BY THE CANADIAN CANNABIS STORE · FOR ADULTS 19+

Starting with cannabis does not have to feel like a test. The best first step is simply being curious, asking questions, and giving yourself room to go slowly.

Start with the moment

Before looking at a menu, think about the setting. Are you at home? With friends? Looking for something to pair with a quiet evening? Sharing that context with a knowledgeable store team can make the conversation much easier.

Read, ask, then decide

Packaging gives you useful basics: format, quantity, cannabinoid information, and the licensed producer. If a term is unfamiliar, ask. A good retail experience should feel clear and pressure-free.

A simple rule of thumb: start low, go slow, and avoid mixing cannabis with alcohol or other substances.

Make a plan

Choose a time when you have no driving, work, or important obligations. Keep water nearby, stay somewhere comfortable, and let the experience unfold at its own pace.

This article is for general education only and is not medical advice. Cannabis affects everyone differently. Adults 19+ only. Do not drive or operate machinery while impaired.
